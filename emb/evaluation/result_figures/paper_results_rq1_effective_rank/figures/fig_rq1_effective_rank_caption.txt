Effective dimensionality of Basin2Vec and the general-purpose EO representations. All methods are evaluated on the same set of 9,066 basins after averaging available samples within each basin and L2-normalizing the resulting descriptors. Bar lengths show effective rank normalized by the maximum attainable rank, min(d,N-1), enabling comparison across representations with different embedding dimensions. Bold values report the normalized effective rank, while the right-hand column reports the absolute effective rank relative to the maximum attainable rank. Higher normalized effective rank indicates greater use of the available representation dimensions and less dimensional collapse.
